Snowboard Hanger

A wall-mounted hanger designed to display a snowboard or a wakeboard as a piece of decor. The mounting arm holds the board securely while keeping it flush and level against the wall.

The faceplate is the focal point, two illustrated variants were printed: a mountain panorama and an ocean-waves graphic, letting the hanger match different aesthetics. Both are multicolour prints produced with Bambu Studio's colour-change workflow.
