Mini Hand Fan

A compact handheld fan designed to be fully self-contained — the handle houses a rechargeable battery and a USB-C charging port, so no separate power bank is needed.

The housing, blade guard, and handle were all modelled in Fusion 360 and printed with Bambu Studio. The electronics are integrated cleanly into the handle geometry without exposed wiring.
