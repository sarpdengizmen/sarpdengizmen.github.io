This is a MakerWorld design contest participation for "Layered with Love: 2026 Valentine's Day Design Contest" which won the Excellent Participation Award.

The idea was to create a design that had motion and element of surprise, while also being useful after gifting. The design needed to be fully 3d printed and assembled easily.
